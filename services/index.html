Services

Consulting and AI support for small construction businesses

Engage us for one thing or the whole lot. Each service stands on its own and fits around the way you already work.

01

Business consulting

Straight answers on the numbers and the systems behind your jobs, so you can price with confidence and stop being surprised at the end of the month.

  • Pricing and marginsWork out what to charge so every job earns what it should.
  • Quoting and job costingFaster, more consistent quotes, and a clear view of actual costs against your estimates.
  • Cash flowInvoicing, payment terms and forward planning so money isn't a surprise.
  • Systems and processesSimple, repeatable ways of working so jobs run the same way every time.
  • Growth planningDecide what kind of work to chase and how to take it on without burning out.
02

AI assistance

AI can take a lot of the writing, checking and chasing off your plate. We show you where it helps, set it up, and train you and your team to use it well. A person always checks the output before it goes to a client.

  • Quotes and proposalsDraft clear, consistent quotes and scopes of work in a fraction of the time.
  • Emails and follow-upsReplies, reminders and chasing overdue invoices, written in your voice.
  • Tender and scope responsesStructure and draft responses so you can tender for more work.
  • Scheduling and job updatesKeep clients and crews informed without extra phone calls.
  • Reports and paperworkTurn rough notes into tidy job reports, checklists and how-to guides for your team.
